FAQ

Answers to the questions most patients ask before booking their first visit.

Is my consultation free?

+

Consultations under 15 minutes are free. If your case needs a longer, more detailed look, that time is charged at the clinic's standard consultation fee — we'll let you know before it happens.

Either way, you'll leave with a clear picture of your options.

How long does treatment take?

+

Most treatments take between 6 and 24 months, depending on complexity. Simple aligner cases can finish in as little as 4–6 months. Complex adult cases with skeletal work take longer.

We'll give you a specific timeline at your consultation once we've seen your scans and X-rays.

Are clear aligners as effective as braces?

+

For most cases — yes. Modern aligners handle the vast majority of orthodontic problems, including many that were considered "braces only" a decade ago. Our shape-memory aligners are especially strong on rotations and torque movements.

For very complex skeletal cases or severe crowding, we may still recommend braces as the better tool. Both options are on the table.

I'm an adult — is it too late for me?

+

Not at all. Around half of our patients are adults. Teeth respond to orthodontic forces at any age, and modern treatment is nearly invisible.

Whether you want to fix something that's always bothered you, prepare for cosmetic dental work, or retreat a case that relapsed years ago — we can help.

Can you show me what my smile will look like?

+

Yes — this is one of the best parts of modern orthodontics. Using an intraoral scanner and digital smile design software, we generate a simulation of your finished smile before treatment begins.

You see the result on screen. If something doesn't feel right, we adjust the plan until it does — before a single tooth moves.

What does treatment cost?

+

Cost depends entirely on the complexity of your case and the treatment chosen. A short aligner case is very different from a full braces-and-surgery plan.

At your consultation, you'll receive a written estimate for your specific case, and we can talk through payment plans if that's helpful. No surprises, no hidden fees.

Do you accept referrals from other dentists?

+

Absolutely. We work closely with general dentists, paediatric colleagues, and maxillofacial surgeons across the region. Please refer patients with any existing records — scans, X-rays, or photographs.

For complex or interdisciplinary cases, we're happy to consult before you refer.
